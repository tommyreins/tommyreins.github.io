**Privacy Policy**

**Effective Date:** May 21, 2026

**Step 10 Today** (the “App”) is an iOS application developed and published by Tommy Reins (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information in connection with the App.

We are fully committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to collect **no information** from you at any time.

### 1. Information We Collect
The App collects **no personal data**, **no usage data**, **no device information**, **no location data**, **no health data**, **no analytics**, and **no identifiers** of any kind.

- No account creation or login is required.
- No data is transmitted to our servers or to any third-party servers.
- No tracking or cross-app tracking occurs.
- All functionality operates locally on your device.

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to link to your identity, nothing to sell, and nothing to share.

### 2. Use of Information
Since we collect no information, we do not use any data for any purpose.

### 3. Sharing or Disclosure of Information
We do not share, sell, rent, or disclose any information because none is collected.

### 4. Data Storage and Security
No user data is stored by the App or by Tommy Reins. Any information that exists on your device (such as local preferences or progress saved only on your iPhone) is managed entirely by Apple’s iOS and is not accessible to us.

### 5. Children’s Privacy
The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because we collect no information from any user, we also collect none from children. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by default.

### 6. Your Rights
Because no data is collected:
- There is no data for you to access, correct, delete, or export.
- You do not need to submit a data request.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy within the App and update the “Effective Date” at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

### 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer:

Developer of Step 10 Today  

You may reach out via the App’s support feature (if available) or through public channels associated with the developer.

By using Step 10 Today, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy.

Thank you for using an app that respects your privacy by design.
